Standards

Technical references include EN 115, related national standards and guidance for escalators and moving walkways. The applicable version and requirements should be reviewed for each project.

EN 115, Iranian national standards 13836-1 and 13836-2, related technical guidance, and Chapter 15 of the national building regulations are among the references used when reviewing escalators and moving walkways. The applicable version and scope are checked for each project.

EN 115-1:2017 covers the safety of construction and installation of new escalators and moving walks. EN 115-2:2021 addresses improvements to the safety of existing equipment.

APTA RT-EE-RP-001-02 (2019 edition) provides guidance on specifications for heavy-duty escalators in public transit. It is design guidance and does not replace local safety requirements.

ISO 25745-3:2015 describes methods for estimating energy use and classifying the energy performance of escalators and moving walks.
