Engineering Guide to Escalator Installation

From pit measurements to checks before commissioning: a practical guide for coordinating architecture, structure, power and installation.

In this guide

  1. 1. Project inputs
  2. 2. Pit and opening survey
  3. 3. Structure and supports
  4. 4. Power and safety coordination
  5. 5. Installation and commissioning

Final pit and support dimensions must come from the selected model’s installation drawing, manufacturer instructions and the responsible engineer’s approval. This guide is not a construction drawing.

01

1. Project inputs

Record finished floor rise, building use, passenger flow, required step width, travel direction and the selected model’s permitted angle. Plan clear entry and exit areas.

02

2. Pit and opening survey

Measure pit length and width at several points and levels. Check wall plumb, diagonals and floor level. Compare lower pit depth, upper headroom, opening length and support spacing with the installation drawing. Record structural and finished dimensions separately.

03

3. Structure and supports

Align the escalator centerline and upper and lower truss bearing points across architectural and structural drawings. Verify load capacity, level tolerances, anchors and the delivery route before building the pit.

04

4. Power and safety coordination

Coordinate power supply, earthing, wiring, controls and service access with the manufacturer’s drawings. Review landing space, guards, lighting, waterproofing and drainage where applicable.

05

5. Installation and commissioning

Survey the completed structure again. Qualified personnel should handle lifting, positioning, anchoring, adjustment and tests of brakes and protective devices according to manufacturer instructions. Retain test records and as-built drawings.

Prepare these for an initial review

  • Architectural plan and section with finished levels
  • Measured pit, opening and landing dimensions
  • Structural support drawings and loads
  • Selected model and manufacturer installation drawing
  • Power information, delivery route and site conditions
