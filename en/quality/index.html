Quality documents

Tests, certificates and approvals related to the products

Quality-control equipment is used for step torsion and pressure tests, step dynamics, chain tension, handrail tear and fabric tests, and compound hardness and pressure tests. A data logger records test data.

The company’s escalators are manufactured in accordance with guidance from the American Public Transportation Association (APTA), European EN 115 standards and Iranian standard 13836. By producing 100% of escalator components, the company has supplied a large share of the components needed by the country’s metro systems, with approval from Sharif University of Technology. That university’s approval for these products supports this statement.
